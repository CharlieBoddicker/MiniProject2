TF Encrypted showed a declining activity pattern and had several different periods of inactivity. Before its longest gap, many of the commits involved TensorFlow compatibility, bug fixes, and installation-related problems. I think these compatibility and maintenance difficulties may have contributed to the project slowing down, although there was not one commit that clearly explained the gap.

The project did return after the gap, but only for two bug-fix commits from a new contributor. Because the recovery was so limited and the project later became inactive again, I do not think this was a successful long-term recovery. The pattern suggests that maintaining compatibility became increasingly difficult and development gradually declined until the project was eventually abandoned.
